Core question

Even if a competitive-integrity violation occurred, do the rules, organizer instructions, event design, investigation process and disclosed reasoning adequately justify permanent account bans and lifetime exclusion from official PUBG esports?

Strongest public record

  1. KRAFTON concluded that Himass and TanVuu used external information / stream sniping after reviewing official broadcasts, participant footage, in-game data and replays. This is KRAFTON's official finding, not an independent finding by this archive.
  2. KRAFTON permanently banned both PUBG accounts and permanently revoked official PUBG esports eligibility.
  3. KRAFTON also acknowledged operational and communication responsibility and said it was reviewing event management, announcements, investigation and decision-making.
  4. ChosunBiz reported that the pre-controversy rulebook recommended an appropriate stream delay but did not mandate a specific delay or require live-chat restrictions comparable with some other PUBG competitions.
  5. GAM x TE challenged proportionality; Anyone's Legend said it was reviewing the rules, rulebook, tournament management and instructions delivered to Himass in support of appeal rights.
  6. Team Korea boycotted Day 2. Inven Global reported a dispute over what KRAFTON had told the Korean side the Vietnamese apology would contain.
  7. The event ended early and some sponsors paused related activity while waiting for KRAFTON's response.

Required distinctions

  • Unsolicited information appearing in chat ≠ deliberately seeking that information.
  • Seeing outside information ≠ necessarily using it in tactical decisions.
  • A violation finding ≠ automatic proof that a lifetime sanction is proportionate.
  • KRAFTON saying other participants were investigated ≠ a publicly reproducible investigation.
  • Allegations concerning Soopi ≠ proof that Soopi cheated.

User-supplied leads that must NOT be stated as fact yet

  • Alleged organizer authorization that 'stream checking / đá stream' was allowed — original clip/message and timestamp not yet archived.
  • Claim that viewers independently pushed positions/stream links into Himass/TanVuu's chat without the players actively requesting most of the information — full POV/chat evidence pending.
  • Claim that the players initially did not want to apologize and were encouraged by organizers to do so to de-escalate the showmatch — original evidence pending.
  • DJ Chip allegedly described internal opposition in KRAFTON Vietnam and Korea to permanent bans, with a higher-level decision ultimately imposed — original clip/timestamp pending.
  • Issacc / AL-management testimony allegedly corroborates parts of DJ Chip's backstage account — original source pending.
  • Claim that the permanent-ban outcome was effectively fixed or heavily directed very early — not yet supported by archived primary evidence.
  • Soopi-related allegations involving Input Director, multiple monitors/reflections, direct stream viewing and possible aim assistance remain unproven allegations.
  • The theory that the event was a deliberate pre-planned setup targeting Himass/TanVuu is not established by the current public record.

最强的公开记录

  1. KRAFTON 表示，在审查官方转播、参赛者视频、游戏内数据和回放后，认定 Himass 与 TanVuu 使用了外部信息/直播狙击。这是 KRAFTON 的官方结论，不是本档案独立做出的结论。
