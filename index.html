PUBG Asia Stars 2026 Case Archive

Independent, multilingual, source-first archive of the Himass–TanVuu controversy, KRAFTON’s sanctions, event rules and unresolved questions.

This archive does not ask readers to assume either side is correct. It separates verified facts, official claims, testimony, allegations and analysis.

Central question / Câu hỏi trung tâm / 核心问题 / คำถามหลัก / 핵심 질문

Even if a competitive-integrity violation occurred, do the published rules, event design, investigation process and disclosed reasoning adequately explain and justify permanent account bans and lifetime exclusion from official PUBG esports?

Core public record

  • KRAFTON said on 23 September 2026 that, after reviewing official broadcasts, participant footage, in-game data and replays, it found Himass and TanVuu had used out-of-game information / stream sniping and imposed permanent sanctions.
  • KRAFTON also acknowledged responsibility for failures in event operation and communication and said it was reviewing operations, communications, investigation and decision-making.
  • In an earlier apology, PUBG Publishing head Yoon Su-jin said KRAFTON had not clearly established preventive measures, judgment criteria or sanction levels in advance for the use of outside-game information.
  • The official event materials describe Asia Stars as a three-day, six-region competition mixing professional players, PUBG Partners and streamers in creative modes.
  • GAM x TE challenged the proportionality and handling of the permanent sanction; Anyone’s Legend said it was reviewing rules and procedures to support Himass’s appeal rights.

  • Tài liệu sự kiện chính thức mô tả Asia Stars là cuộc thi ba ngày, sáu khu vực, có tuyển thủ chuyên nghiệp, PUBG Partner và streamer, với nhiều chế độ sáng tạo.
  • GAM x TE phản đối tính tương xứng/cách xử lý án vĩnh viễn; Anyone’s Legend cho biết rà soát quy định và thủ tục để hỗ trợ quyền kháng nghị của Himass.

PUBG 亚洲明星赛 2026 事件档案

独立、多语言、以来源为核心的档案，整理 Himass–TanVuu 争议、KRAFTON 处罚、赛事规则及尚未解决的问题。

Central question / Câu hỏi trung tâm / 核心问题 / คำถามหลัก / 핵심 질문

即使确实存在竞技诚信违规，已公布的规则、赛事设计、调查程序与公开理由，是否足以解释并支持永久封禁账号和终身禁止参加官方 PUBG 电竞赛事？

Core public record

  • 2026年9月23日，KRAFTON 表示在审查官方转播、参赛者视频、游戏内数据和回放后，认定 Himass 与 TanVuu 使用了游戏外信息/直播狙击，并实施永久处罚。
  • KRAFTON 同时承认赛事运营与沟通存在责任，并表示将审查运营、沟通、调查与决策全过程。
  • 更早的道歉声明中，PUBG Publishing 负责人 Yoon Su-jin 表示，KRAFTON 事前没有明确建立针对游戏外信息使用的预防措施、判断标准与处罚等级。
  • 官方赛事资料把 Asia Stars 描述为为期三天、六个地区、职业选手、PUBG Partner 与主播共同参与的创意模式赛事。
  • 据 Inven Global 报道，GAM x TE 质疑永久处罚的比例性与处理方式；Anyone’s Legend 表示正在审查规则和程序，以支持 Himass 的申诉权利。
